1. **Page introduction.** "Serving the people of Moraine Township" is now
   "serving the **residents** of Moraine Township", and the second sentence
   continues "...provide assistance **with your property assessment, assessment
   appeals and property tax exemptions**", both as handwritten.
2. **Welcome paragraph.** "...provide you with the information available at our
   office" is now "...provide you with **pertinent information about your
   property assessment process and exemptions you may qualify for**." The
   sentence about records being public information stays.

## The tutorial video (Tom's emailed request)

The video Tom pointed to on lakecountyil.gov under Assessment Notices is the
Chief County Assessment Office's **"Assessment Appeal Tutorial for the
E-Filing System"**, published on the county's official LakeCountyTV YouTube
channel (video id `sm45jCEb2mI`; the county page links that exact video, which
was verified in a browser on August 28, 2026).

It is now embedded on the page under **"How to appeal your assessment
online"**, with a click-to-play poster so no YouTube scripts load until a
resident presses play, a plain "Watch the tutorial on YouTube" link as a
fallback, and translated labels on the Spanish and Russian pages, which also
note the video itself is in English.
