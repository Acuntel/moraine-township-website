STATE HEALTH INSURANCE PROGRAM (SHIP) Assistance enrolling in Medicare To schedule an appointment for either program with a Counselor, call 847-432-3240 or email info@morainetownship.org THIS SERVICE IS FREE Moraine Township has trained and certified Counselors to confidentially help residents explore their health insurance options on the Federal Marketplace. You may be eligible for subsidies to help you afford health insurance You may be eligible for reduced out-of-pocket expenses You may be eligible for expanded Medicaid For 2026 health insurance, enrollment starts November 1, 2025. Trained and certified Counselors are available to work one-on-one with residents to help them understand their options, balance their medical needs and budgets, compare coverage and costs, and ensure that enrollees obtain the premium subsidies and cost-sharing reductions they may be income-qualified to receive. A Counselor will help enrollees learn about changes that may have taken place in their existing plan, or review details and compare costs of alternative plans. Health insurance offered on GetCoveredIllinois Marketplace under the ACA addresses the gaps many of our citizens experience when their employer does not offer this benefit, they are self-employed, unemployed, recently divorced, retired early, or a myriad of reasons people just don’t have access to health care.
