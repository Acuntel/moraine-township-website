Latino Advisory Committee members collaborate to inform residents of services available from Moraine Township inform Township Trustees of gaps in human services the Township may be able to help address. The Board of Trustees wishes to foster grassroots government participation by establishing a welcoming atmosphere for the Latino community. Calendar The committee meets periodically, usually on weekday evenings. If you would like to receive information on meeting dates and agendas, email info@morainetownship.org to be added to the distribution list. The schedule is posted on the website Home Page. If you would like to find out more about joining the committee, please fill out a volunteer application ( click here ) and someone will get back to you with more information. Thanks to members of the Latino Advisory Committee who do so much to help our residents.
