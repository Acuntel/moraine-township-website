The program is offered through the IRS Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA). Volunteers are trained and certified by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to prepare basic returns for taxpayers earning less than $67,000. Volunteers will be available Saturdays between 8:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m. beginning January 31, for taxpayers to drop off their tax documents at the Township office, 800 Central Avenue, Highland Park, and to pick up their finished return on a subsequent Saturday. Tax return preparation is handled remotely; volunteers will review taxpayer documents and call taxpayers with questions if necessary. To participate in the program, visit Moraine Township, 800 Central Avenue, Highland Park, Saturdays between 8:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m. or leave a message at 847-780-6644; a certified volunteer will return your call. Please complete this form before your appointment (English 13614-C or Spanish 13614-C (SP) and bring it to your appointment. You must also bring with you a photo ID for both the taxpayer and spouse and original Social Security card(s) or ITIN letter(s) for each taxpayer and dependent listed on the return. Click here for the original tax documents you need to bring for preparation of your income tax return.
