Rachel graduated with Bachelor's degrees in Accountancy and Supply Chain Management from the University of Illinois. She graduated from the Harvard Kennedy School with her Masters in Public Policy degree where she concentrated in Social and Urban Policy. Rachel works in the gun violence prevention movement and previously served as an English teacher on a Fulbright grant in Malaysia. She has served on the Moraine Township Grant Committee since 2024.
