If you are eligible for this exemption and it is not reflected on your tax bill, please contact Moraine Township Assessor office 847-432-2100. Homestead Improvement Exemption If an improvement or addition is made to the home, the assessment increase due to the alteration is kept off the tax rolls for four years. There is a maximum of $25,000 of assessed value ($75,000 of market value) which can be kept off the tax rolls for that period. Returning veterans, Disabled and Veterans Exemptions For details, consult the Chief County Assessment Officer links. New Property Owner If you purchased your home within the past year, call us to discuss exemptions you may be eligible for or if the Township’s value is higher than your purchase price. Commercial Property Vacancies If you have a vacant commercial property, a vacancy reduction on the property is not automatically granted. Supporting data is required for a vacancy reduction to be considered. Call us or click https://www.lakecountyil.gov/DocumentCenter/View/40241/2021-Board-of-Review-Vacancy-Affadavit for a copy of the Township’s vacancy guidelines and vacancy affidavit.
