Upcoming Elections Tuesday June 28, 2022 General Primary – Nomination of Federal, State and County candidates by an established political party Tuesday November 8, 2022 General Election – Election of Federal, State and County officials Early Voting Voters may cast their ballots beginning on the Monday fifteen days before the election and extending through the Saturday before Election Day. Early voting is available at sites that are designated by the Lake County Clerk’s office. Call 847-377-2406 or visit Early Voting Locations Vote by Mail Any Illinois voter is eligible to vote by mail. Voters may vote by mail as early as 40 days before an election. Send an email to VotingByMail@lakecountyil.gov , or call 847-377-2406, to request an application to vote by mail. Polling Place Information Your polling place is determined by the precinct in which you live. To locate your Election Day voting site, visit Election Day Voting Site All polling places are open from 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM on Election Day.
