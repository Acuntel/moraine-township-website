By law, voter registration is closed for 28 days before each election and resumes shortly after the election. To check your voter registration status, visit the Lake County Clerk’s Voter Power site or call 847-377-2410. REGISTER TO VOTE ONLINE If you have an Illinois driver’s license or state ID you may register online through the Illinois State Board of Elections . To confirm your voter registration status visit Lake County Voter Power .
