All income-qualified Moraine Township residents are eligible to visit the Food Pantry. Download a Food Pantry Application here - English Application or Spanish Application or Russian Application Questions about Moraine Township Food Pantry should be directed to Giselle Valle 847-432-3240 - coordinator@morainetownship.org Food pantry hours: Monday: 2:00-4:00 pm Tuesday: 10 am to noon Wednesdays: 1:30 pm to 3:30 pm Pantry visitors receive produce, dairy, meat, bread, shelf-stable food; SNAP recipients receive household goods and personal care products when available. Download a Food Pantry Flyer (pdf) We appreciate your support of the food pantry: FOOD MOST NEEDED Please bring donations of unopened, unexpired nonperishable food and household essentials to the Township office M-F 8:30 am to 4:30 pm Donate Volunteer at the Pantry Volunteer at our Food Pantry Garden The Pantry Elm Place Middle Schoolers Levi Goldberg and Andrew Kourkine produced this documentary on our food pantry in 2017.
