Call 847-432-3240 for information about the utility assistance programs we may be able to connect you with. LIHEAP (Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program) The Illinois LIHEAP program available to many eligible low-income households pay for winter energy services. Application dates are typically September through May unless funding is exhausted sooner. To learn more about the program and eligibility, call 847-432-3240. Share the Warmth Share the Warmth is a North Shore Gas program administered by Community Action Partnership of Lake County (CAP) available to qualified low-income eligible residents. To learn more about the program and eligibility, call 847-432-3240. Com Ed Hardship The ComEd Hardship program is available to residents who are past due on their utility bill and meet the qualifications of the program. To learn more about the program and eligibility, call 847-432-3240. Moraine Township Summer Utility Assistance Moraine Township offers summer utility assistance for income-qualified senior and disabled residents living in a stand-alone structure; the program is not available to residents who live in buildings with access to a cooling room in the building. To learn more about the program and eligibility, call 847-432-3240.
