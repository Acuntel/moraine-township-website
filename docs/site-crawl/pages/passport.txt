APPLICATIONS ACCEPTED Moraine Township 800 Central Avenue | Highland Park Call 847-432-3240 to schedule an appointment Appointments are accepted Monday through Friday 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. To successfully complete your application, please bring: Completed DS-11 New Passport Application – do not sign the application Completed DS-82 Renewal Application - do not sign the application One Passport Photo (we can take your photo! - $15.00 charge) Certified copy of birth certificate, Certificate of Naturalization, or expired passport Photo ID –Drivers’ License, State ID, Military ID Payment in check or money order only If passport is for a child (under age 16), both parents must be present Visit travel.state.gov or morainetownship.org to learn what to bring to the appointment.
