Moraine Township is here to help residents in need of extra support. General Assistance (GA) and Emergency Assistance (EA) are state-regulated functions of the Office of the Supervisor. Qualifications for assistance are regulated by Illinois statute. Information on General Assistance applicants is strictly confidential.To learn more about the programs and eligibility, call Case Worker Linda Contreras 847-432-3240 . Emergency Assistance Our Emergency Assistance program provides Moraine Township residents with assistance in alleviating a life-threatening circumstance or job-related expense. Residents experiencing a hardship due to a current emergency such as loss of income, reduction in hours, medical illness, among others are encouraged to apply. General Assistance The state mandated General Assistance program provides Moraine Township residents assistance up to $1800 towards monthly basic living expenses such as rent, utilities, & food. You may be eligible for General Assistance if you are unemployed and actively seeking employment, or are currently awaiting a decision on a social security disability claim. Applicants must meet state-mandated qualifications.
