Click here for photos of our volunteers Contact Sara Hoffman 847-432-3240 volunteer@morainetownship.org Moraine Township is grateful for volunteers who make it possible for us to provide expanded services to residents! Click here for Volunteer Application Moraine Township Food Pantry Minimum volunteer age is 16. Sort, shelve, clean up, assist shoppers, pick up food contributions at area stores. Regular weekly volunteers needed as well as substitutes and occasional volunteers for special events. Volunteer for a 2-hour shift the first weekend in December at Sunset Foods to raise funds for the Food Pantry. Many volunteers needed to cover all shifts for the 3-day event. Postal Workers’ Food Drive: 2nd Saturday in May. Many volunteers needed to help unload and sort food. Host a food drive any time of the year at your convenience. Holiday Gift Drive Residents sponsor community children and seniors to provide holiday gifts and cheer for HP/HW families and seniors. Free Income Tax Return Preparation Train with the Internal Revenue Service online during December and January to prepare free tax returns for low-income residents from February to April. Health Insurance Enrollment Assistance Train with Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services(CMS) online during September and October to help residents without health insurance to sign-up on the Federal Marketplace during open enrollment. Citizen Advisory Committees Volunteers participate on our Latino Advisory Committee, Citizen Advisory Grant Committee, and other ad hoc committees, as needed. Email info@morainetownship.org if you are interested. The Food Pantry garden We grow produce for our Food Pantry.
