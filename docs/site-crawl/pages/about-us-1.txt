Moraine Township is a “special purpose” governmental unit providing: General Assistance to individuals Property assessment services in the Assessor ’s Office Election and record-keeping functions in the Clerk ’s Office Management of Cemeteries : Mooney and Daggitt Other Resident Services: Click here for a complete list of Moraine Township services Additional information on Township functions can be found at Townships of Illinois . Geographic Boundaries Located in southeast Lake County along the Lake Michigan shore, Moraine Township and the municipalities within its borders are not co-terminus. Through the years new municipalities incorporated and existing municipalities annexed and expanded into other Townships. Moraine Township includes most of Highland Park, all of Highwood, and parts of southeast Lake Forest and east Deerfield.
